# tookan ($TOOKAN)

**Fly to a billion.** A toucan with a sunset, a neon road and no sense of direction.

**Live site:** https://useliving.github.io/tookan/  
**X:** https://x.com/UseTookan

## What's on the site
- A full-screen synthwave sunset with a toucan running down a rainbow road, dancing, moonwalking and faceplanting in time with an original beat
- Click the bird to make it squawk; your squawk count is saved in your browser
- Meme gallery, tookan radio playlist (links out to Spotify and YouTube), and how to buy

Single self-contained `index.html`.
